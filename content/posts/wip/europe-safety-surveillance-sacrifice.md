> "Those who would give up essential liberty to purchase a little temporary safety, deserve neither liberty nor safety." ~ Benjamin Franklin

After the absolute political clusterfuck that was the passing of Chat Control, which through [a legal loophole]() managed to pass despite an overwhelming majority against, for many, this has been a rough wakeup call at the state of the European democracy.

Furthermore, the once heavily [pro-privacy]() EU has been seemingly walking back on it's promises, and the new drive to [create a fully virtualized Euro]() is just another step in the path to total surveillance.
